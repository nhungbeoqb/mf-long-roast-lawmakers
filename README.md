One satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto
or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF. `main.py` writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` (226 characters, one sentence).

> The senator who wrote the AI safety bill still emails his staff a Word
> file called FINAL_final_v7 and asks them to print it, sign it, scan it
> back, and explain why the PDF button keeps hiding from him, preferably
> before lunch.
